The 5 Audit Exceptions It Flags — and Why Each One Ruins a Friday Afternoon

TickTieLocal doesn't hand you vague AI guesses. It runs deterministic invoice-number and amount reconciliation against your CSV rows and classifies every single item into one of five plain-English audit buckets. You can watch it catch all five in under 500ms using the built-in sample engagement:

EXACT_MATCH

Cleared in milliseconds. Ledger invoice number and total match the supporting PDF to the cent (e.g., AWS INV-2026-101 at $1,250.00). No squinting across split screens just to tick a clean line.

AMOUNT_VARIANCE

Wrong number keyed into AP. The invoice ID matches, but the bookkeeper mistyped a digit, missed VAT, or used a stale FX rate. In our live sample, Hetzner INV-2026-102 is booked at 480.50 while the German PDF reads Gesamtbetrag: 525,50 € — a €45 variance caught automatically.

MISSING_PDF

Ledger line with zero paperwork. A $2,400.00 Gamma Legal LLP entry sits in the books, but nobody saved the PDF invoice in the audit folder. That is the exact line an external reviewer or tax auditor pulls first.

DUPLICATE_LEDGER

Posted twice, paid twice. Same invoice number (INV-2026-101, $1,250.00) entered on two separate ledger rows. Catching one duplicate AP posting before a wire run pays for the entire quarter.

UNRECORDED_PDF

Cutoff liability hiding in the folder. A real vendor bill (delta-freight-999.pdf, $320.00) landed in the client folder but never made it into the AP CSV. Classic unrecorded-liability error caught before close.

Be Honest: Who Should NOT Use TickTieLocal?

A tiny offline tool cannot replace a multi-million-dollar audit platform for every firm. Save yourself ten minutes and skip TickTieLocal if either of these describes your workload:

  • Half your client folder is crooked phone photos of crumpled paper receipts. TickTieLocal extracts selectable text from native digital PDFs (the kind Stripe, AWS, Hetzner, law firms, and SaaS vendors email out). It does not run cloud OCR on flat image scans. If you are buried in photographed paper, pay for DataSnipper, Dext, or AutoEntry.
  • You manage 50 assurance staff who need deep Excel cell-level snip links. DataSnipper lives inside Excel workbooks and ties highlighted PDF coordinates straight to spreadsheet cells for large multi-preparer sign-off chains. TickTieLocal is a standalone browser matcher + CSV workpaper exporter, not a heavy Excel COM add-in.
  • Who TickTieLocal IS built for: Small CPA firms (1–15 people), internal controllers, and fractional CFOs who work with clean digital vendor PDFs + an AP/AR CSV export, care deeply about client confidentiality, and want five-second exception answers without an enterprise sales cycle.
