Honest Caveat: Who Should NOT Use LocalLedger?

Save yourself five minutes before dropping files in. LocalLedger does one job well and intentionally skips OCR:

  • Scanned paper statements or phone photos: LocalLedger extracts selectable text from native digital PDFs downloaded straight from online banking portals. Drop in a flat scan or photographed paper statement and it will stop with SCANNED_PDF_DETECTED instead of hallucinating digits. For paper scans, buy Dext or DocuClipper.
